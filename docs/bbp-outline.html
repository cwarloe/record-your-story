TITLE
Your Memories, Captured with AI
A personal timeline app that uses AI to turn spoken and written memories into shareable events.
ACT 1: SETUP
Setting Role
You have stories in journals, files, photos, and your head, and they're easy to lose. You're a family historian, journaler, or storyteller who wants to capture and share them.
Point A (Where am I?) Call to Action (How do I get from A to B?) Point B (Where do I want to be?)
Typing every memory into a timeline is slow, and old journals sit unread. Try the demo, speak or import your first events, and invite family. A life story that lasts and the family can share.
